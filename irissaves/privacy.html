Privacy Policy – Iris Videos Uploader

Last updated: November 7, 2026

What this app is

Iris Videos Uploader is a private, internal tool. It is used only by the owner of the Iris Saves YouTube channel (and the owner's other channels) to upload videos to those channels and to read those channels' own statistics. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data the app accesses

Through Google's OAuth and the YouTube API Services, with the channel owner's permission, the app accesses only:

  • Basic information about the owner's own YouTube channels (name and ID), to confirm the right channel is selected.
  • The ability to upload videos, thumbnails and caption files to those channels, and to set their title, description, tags and publishing time.
  • YouTube Analytics reports for those channels' own videos (for example views, watch time and audience retention).

The app does not access any other user's data, comments, subscribers' personal information, Gmail, Drive or contacts.

How data is stored and used

OAuth tokens and analytics reports are stored only on the owner's private server, with access restricted to the owner. The data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to improve future videos. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Deleting data

Revoking access in Google security settings stops the app immediately. Stored tokens and reports are deleted by the owner on request or when no longer needed.
